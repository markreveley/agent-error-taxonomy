# Direct-check omission

## Definition

A precise mechanism, number, API shape, command, environment fact,
implementation behavior, date, or resource status is stated without a cheap,
directly available check.

## Inclusion criteria

- The claim is concrete and externally checkable.
- A direct source, runtime, help, code, link, or arithmetic check is readily
  available.
- The direct check materially disproves the claim.

## Exclusions and contrast cases

- Use `epistemic.evidence.scope_omission` for broad coverage claims.
- Use `epistemic.claim.synthesis_as_evidence` when the primary defect is false
  attribution of synthesis to a source.
- An unverified claim is not enough by itself; accepted incidents require a
  comparator that establishes the claim was wrong.
