The thread documents retain the substantive user and assistant messages
verbatim while omitting tool calls, tool results, hidden reasoning, and short
pre-tool narration according to the source repository's session-capture policy.
They are therefore primary evidence for conversational claims, instructions,
corrections, and completion reports, but they cannot by themselves establish
the content of omitted tool results.

Four local raw JSONL sessions also exist in the source workstation's Claude
project history. They cover only a small portion of the repository's history and
are used as overlap checks where applicable; they are not substituted for the
143 persisted thread documents.
