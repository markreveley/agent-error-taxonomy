# Distinction collapse

## Definition

Materially different mechanisms, units, speech acts, criteria domains, or
consequences are treated as equivalent or inseparable.

## Inclusion criteria

- The relevant facts are available rather than missing.
- The agent conflates two or more materially different categories.
- Restoring the distinction changes the answer, recommendation, classification,
  or consequence.

## Exclusions and contrast cases

- Use an `epistemic.*` type when the distinction was missed because the relevant
  evidence was not acquired.
- A merely omitted design alternative without a controlling invariant or
  objective comparator is not enough to establish this error.
- Simplification is not a collapse when the omitted distinction cannot change
  the task-relevant conclusion.
