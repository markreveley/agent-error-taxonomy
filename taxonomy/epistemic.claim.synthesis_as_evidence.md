# Synthesis presented as evidence

## Definition

Agent- or tool-generated synthesis is presented as though it were a recorded
definition, primary-source statement, or directly supported quantitative fact.

## Inclusion criteria

- The claim's provenance is material to its credibility.
- The underlying source does not contain the asserted formulation or number.
- The synthesis is not labeled as inference, interpretation, or estimate.

## Exclusions and contrast cases

- Use `epistemic.source.authority_misuse` when a stale or weak source actually
  contains the claim.
- Use `reasoning.distinction.collapse` when the problem is conceptual conflation
  independent of attribution.
- Clearly labeled synthesis or inference is outside this type unless it also
  makes a separately false factual claim.

## Examples

- `AET-EM-0027` — confirmed
- `AET-EM-0038` — probable
